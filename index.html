Jiaqi Zhang (张家祺)

Jiaqi (Jacky) Zhang is a PhD student at The University of Queensland (UQ), under the supervision of Prof. Hongzhi Yin, Prof. Rocky Tong Chen and Prof. Junliang Yu. His research interests lie in Large Language Models, Personalization, Recommender Systems, LLM Reasoning, and LLM Agents. He did a research internship at Tsinghua University, working under the guidance of Prof. Yong Li and Prof. Chen Gao. He served as a research assistant at Westlake University, advised by Prof. Fajie Yuan.
